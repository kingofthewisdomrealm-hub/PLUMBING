PLUMBING

The whole plumbing system of a Treasure Coast CBS house, as a model you can spin. Drains, vents, hot and cold, gas, and the fixtures the owner touches — laid in the order a plumber actually lays them, with the seven Indian River County inspections standing between you and the next step. Training — not a permit.

Two models, one house. Rough to Trim builds the plumbing — three visits, seven county inspections. The Fixer comes back to the same house five years later, when the phone rings. Build it right in the first one and you will recognise every fault in the second one, because four of them are mistakes the first model warns you about.

Six calls a plumber actually gets — a toilet that won’t stop, a sink that won’t drain, no hot water by the second shower, a pipe dripping by the heater, a guest bath that stinks, and a house backing up. Listen, look, find, fix, prove. Every card carries what the work goes for on the Treasure Coast, and every call has a shortcut that works today and costs you the whole job twice.
